## 2. Market: the form says MIX

타겟 국가/시장 is `MIX`, and 타겟 국가 is **"현재 더운 날씨를 가진 국가 대상 (인도/동남아/중동/호주권)"** —
India, Southeast Asia, the Middle East and Australia.

The Market dropdown holds one value, and of the hot-weather markets it offers Vietnam,
Thailand, Indonesia, Malaysia, Singapore and the Philippines. Two honest ways to handle it:

- **One campaign, lead market in the dropdown** (suggested): put your strongest SEA market in
  Market, and keep the full list in the note. Simplest, and the roster stays one list.
- **One campaign per market** if you want per-market reporting later — more setup, cleaner
  numbers, and each one gets its own shortlist.

A cooling cream sells on felt heat, so I would weight the shortlist toward **Indonesia,
Vietnam, Thailand, the Philippines, India and the UAE** rather than spreading evenly.

## 3. Who to shortlist

From the brief rather than from habit:

- **Platform:** Instagram only (희망 플랫폼). Reels is the required format, so look at whether
  their Reels carry, not their grid.
- **Content fit:** skincare and "get ready with me" creators — the product's two strongest
  claims are a cooling sensation and makeup that sits better on cooled skin, both of which show
  on camera. A creator who only does flat-lay product photography cannot show either.
- **Markets:** hot-weather countries as above. A Korean-market creator is off-brief here.
- **Size:** mid-tier. The KPI is awareness *and* conversion, and ₩32,000 of product is a
  gifting-level ask — micro and mid creators accept gifting, large ones usually do not.
- **Language:** the required mention is that it is **buyable on 스타일코리안닷컴**, which is an
  export storefront. Creators whose audience already buys K-beauty that way convert; purely
  domestic-Korean audiences do not.
- **Exclude:** anyone flagged blacklisted or caution — the dashboard marks them on the roster.

Two things in there are worth saying out loud to every creator before they accept, because they
are the ones that cause arguments later:

1. **2차 활용 — the brand says it plans to reuse the content.** The form says 계획 있습니다 and
   nothing about duration, channels or paid amplification. Get those three from the brand before
   outreach, or the contract has a hole in it.
2. **공동 작업자 등록 (Instagram collab tag) is required** — @hangangseoul must be added as a
   collaborator, not just tagged. Some creators refuse collab posts; ask at outreach, not after.

## 6. Running it, once the roster is built

1. **Outreach** → roster rows move Contacted → Replied → Confirmed as people answer.
2. **Addresses.** The brand ships, so they need name + address + phone for each confirmed
   creator. Those sit on the roster row. Send the brand only the confirmed ones, and send it
   as a list you assembled for that purpose — not a workspace export, which carries far more
   about each creator than a courier needs.
3. **Parcels.** Once the brand gives you tracking numbers, open each confirmed row and fill in
   **Courier**, **Tracking number** and **Sent on** (new, see below). The row moves itself to
   *Shipped*, and the roster header counts "product: 7/20 sent". Anything out more than ten days
   with nothing back is flagged, which is your chase list.
4. **Content** → Submitted → In review → Live, as now.
5. **Report.** KPI is awareness + conversion: reach and views answer the first; the second needs
   StyleKorean to tell you what moved, so agree before launch what they will share and when.

## 7. What the brand still owes you

- 2차 활용 scope: how long, which channels, paid or organic.
- Whether there is a posting window, or any date the content must be live by — the form has none.
- Sample/shipping lead time per market, so the ten-day chase threshold is fair to creators in
  India and the Middle East.
- Whether StyleKorean will share conversion data, and in what form.
